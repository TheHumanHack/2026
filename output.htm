Closing thoughts

Consulting mindset

Think, challenge and consult.

Develop human skills alongside technical skills.

Adapt leadership to the situation.

Client value

Prioritise value over functionality.

Question assumptions and find the why.

Free has a cost. More is not always better.

Delivery habits

Use RAID and RAAIDD to protect delivery.

Think long term.

Treat others as you want to be treated.
